### 🌐 Web Resources 

> 
> ##### Descriptive Network Analysis in R for Social Network Analysis
>
>**🌐 Link:** [https://schochastics.github.io/R4SNA/descriptive/introduction.html](https://schochastics.github.io/R4SNA/descriptive/introduction.html) 
>
><i>Termeh Shafie and David Schoch</i>
>
>"Descriptive network analysis is the task of summarizing the structure of an observed network directly from the data, without assuming any probabilistic model for how the ties came to be. The quantities you compute here are properties of the network you have in front of you: how many nodes and edges it contains, how far apart they are, who sits at its center, which regions hang together."
>
